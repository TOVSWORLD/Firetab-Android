# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss evidence and technical choices
without personal attacks. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience.
Accept correction, give credit, and respect privacy and contributors' time.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, sustained disruption
and publication of private information are not acceptable in issues, reviews,
discussions or other project spaces.

The repository owner, TOVSWORLD, is responsible for project enforcement. For a public
incident, use GitHub's report-content controls or a concise issue without private data.
For confidential harassment or abuse reports, use
[GitHub's private abuse-reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
That channel is handled by GitHub, not a project-maintained private inbox. Do not use
software vulnerability reports for conduct incidents. Maintainers will handle reports
they receive with discretion and avoid promising absolute confidentiality.
Responses may include a correction request, content removal, a warning, temporary
restriction or permanent exclusion according to severity and repeated behavior. The
owner reviews project enforcement appeals raised without private data; confidential
or owner-involved abuse reports should use GitHub’s reporting process. No response-time guarantee is made.

This project-specific policy applies equally to maintainers and contributors.
