# Licensing and provenance

A first-party license selection is awaiting the repository owner's decision. Until it
is recorded here with the complete license texts, no general open-source license is
granted for newly authored code or documentation and external code/doc contributions
are not accepted for merge. Bug reports and public research discussion remain welcome.
Do not infer a license from repository visibility or from the licenses of dependencies.

Third-party files retain upstream licenses and notices. Existing kernel code, copied
source excerpts, historical patches, manifests and inspected vendor-derived material
are not relicensed by any future top-level license. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
Before accepting code, identify its origin and governing license. Before redistributing
binary material, establish the right to do so and fulfill source/notice obligations.

Do not copy vendor binaries, Google services bundles, Crestron firmware or signing keys
into this repository. A publicly downloadable package and a pinned hash do not by
themselves grant redistribution permission. Link to upstream and keep provenance.
